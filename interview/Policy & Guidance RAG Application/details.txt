# Case Study

## Design a Policy & Guidance RAG Application for Internal Risk and Review Teams

这版是按你前面希望的风格来设计的：
不是普通 chatbot，不是纯知识库搜索，而是更贴近 **internal risk / review teams** 的实际工作方式，重点考察候选人是否真正理解：

* enterprise RAG
* complex question answering
* evidence traceability
* policy/guidance interpretation boundaries
* review workflow support
* product / workstream leadership
* 何时需要从 standard RAG 走向 agentic / graph-based orchestration

---

# 1. Interview objective

这套 case study 主要考察 5 个方面：

## A. Product understanding

候选人是否理解这不是一个“万能问答助手”，而是给 internal risk / review teams 用的 **policy and guidance decision-support application**。

## B. Practical RAG design

候选人是否真的懂：

* document ingestion
* parsing
* chunking
* metadata
* retrieval
* reranking
* citations
* evaluation
* ambiguity handling

## C. Complex enterprise query handling

候选人是否能处理：

* multi-document questions
* conflicting guidance
* versioned guidance
* scope/applicability questions
* evidence-backed responses

## D. Judgement on architecture

候选人是否知道：

* 什么时候 standard RAG 足够
* 什么时候需要 query decomposition
* 什么时候适合 agentic / graph RAG
* 什么时候不应过度设计

## E. Workstream lead capability

候选人是否能把这个产品作为一个实际 workstream 来推进，而不是只会讲技术组件。

# 3. Candidate Case Brief

下面这部分可以直接给候选人。

---

## Case Study: Design a Policy & Guidance RAG Application

You are asked to design an internal **Policy & Guidance RAG application** for internal risk and review teams.

The application is intended to help users navigate and apply internal policy and guidance documents across review and challenge workflows. Example document types may include:

* policy standards
* procedural guidance
* review checklists
* control requirements
* governance notes
* FAQs
* implementation guidance
* prior review guidance or interpretation notes

The users of the system may include:

* independent review teams
* risk oversight teams
* validators / reviewers
* SMEs involved in review and challenge
* business users preparing submissions who need to understand applicable guidance

This is **not** a simple chatbot and **not** a generic enterprise search tool.

The application should support an end-to-end workflow where users can:

* ask simple factual questions about policy and guidance
* ask more complex questions requiring multiple documents
* identify what guidance is relevant to a specific scenario
* understand where different guidance documents align or differ
* inspect source evidence and citations behind answers
* distinguish between direct policy statements and model-generated synthesis
* handle ambiguity or insufficient evidence safely

Assume:

* documents exist in PDF, Word, HTML, and wiki-like formats
* documents may have different versions, owners, and effective dates
* some user questions are straightforward lookups, while others are complex and require decomposition
* some future use cases may require combining policy/guidance with a second knowledge base containing case-specific evidence or submissions
* outputs must be explainable, auditable, and trustworthy
* the product should evolve from MVP into a broader internal review support platform over time

Please explain:

1. how you would define the product and key user journeys
2. what the MVP should include
3. how you would design the architecture and RAG workflow
4. how you would handle retrieval, citations, and complex multi-document questions
5. when standard RAG is sufficient and when agentic / graph-based orchestration may be needed
6. what controls, risks, and evaluation approach you would use
7. how you would lead delivery of this workstream

---

# 4. Interviewer opening script

你可以直接这样说：

> We’d like you to think through the design of a Policy & Guidance RAG application for internal risk and review teams.
> This is not intended to be a generic chatbot. It should help users navigate policy and guidance documents, answer complex questions with traceable evidence, and support review and challenge workflows in a controlled and auditable way.
>
> Please take a few minutes to think, then walk us through:
>
> * the product you would build
> * the user workflow
> * the architecture and RAG design
> * how you would handle simple versus complex questions
> * what should be automated versus left to human judgement
> * and how you would deliver this as a practical workstream

# 5. What good candidate coverage should include

理想情况下，候选人应覆盖这些方面：

* 这是一个 **policy/guidance decision-support system**
* 不是 general chat assistant
* 用户问题类型需要分层：lookup、summary、scope guidance、triage guidance、comparison、multi-doc synthesis
* retrieval 要考虑 metadata、versions、effective dates、document type
* 输出要有 citations 和 evidence traceability
* 要区分 direct source statements 与 inferred synthesis
* 要有 ambiguity / insufficient evidence handling
* 复杂问题可能需要 decomposition 或 graph / agentic orchestration
* MVP 要聚焦，不要一上来全做
* 要有 evaluation 和 governance thinking

# 6. Core interview questions

下面这套问题适合 50 分钟。

---

## Q1. Product framing

**How would you define this product, and what problem is it solving for internal risk and review teams?**

### 想听到的点

* 这是 policy/guidance review support product
* 不是 generic chatbot
* 目标是提升 consistency, speed, traceability, coverage
* 不是替代 final human judgement
* 帮助 users find relevant guidance, interpret applicability, compare sources, and identify evidence-backed answers

### Follow-up

* Why is this not just an enterprise search tool?
* Why is this not simply a chatbot over documents?
* What makes this use case different from a normal FAQ assistant?

---

## Q2. User journeys

**What are the main user journeys in this product?**

### 想听到的点

候选人最好能说出不同类型任务，例如：

* factual policy lookup
* relevant guidance discovery
* scope/applicability guidance
* comparison across documents
* validation or review support
* follow-up / missing information identification
* escalation / uncertainty handling

### Follow-up

* Who are the main users?
* What is different between a validator/reviewer and a business user preparing a submission?
* What outputs would users actually want to see in the UI?

---

## Q3. MVP definition

**What would your MVP include, and what would you deliberately leave out?**

### 想听到的点

* MVP focused on one or two core workflows
* strong citation and retrieval quality
* controlled question types
* limited document scope or curated KB
* clear UI for evidence-backed answers
* probably avoid over-complex agentic orchestration in phase 1

### Follow-up

* What would you exclude from phase 1?
* What would be risky to include too early?
* How would you explain MVP boundaries to stakeholders?

---

## Q4. End-to-end architecture

**How would you design the end-to-end architecture and RAG workflow?**

### 想听到的点

* ingestion
* parsing
* normalization
* chunking
* metadata model
* indexing
* retrieval
* reranking
* answer generation
* citations
* logging / feedback / evaluation

### Follow-up

* What are the core data objects?
* How would you handle effective dates and document versions?
* Would you use one index or multiple indexes?
* How would you separate knowledge ingestion from online answering?

---

## Q5. Parsing and chunking

**How would you parse and chunk policy and guidance documents for this use case?**

### 想听到的点

* heading-aware chunking
* preserve section hierarchy
* keep metadata such as doc type, owner, version, effective date
* different strategies for standards, checklists, and FAQs
* avoid losing logical structure

### Follow-up

* Would you use the same chunking strategy for all document types?
* What chunking mistakes would hurt citation quality?
* How would you handle tables, annexes, appendices, or embedded lists?

---

## Q6. Retrieval and citations

**How would you design retrieval and evidence traceability?**

### 想听到的点

* dense or hybrid retrieval
* metadata filtering
* reranking
* top-k strategy
* citation mapping to title/section/page/anchor
* distinguish source-backed content from synthesis
* don’t allow unsupported citations

### Follow-up

* How would you stop the model from citing irrelevant retrieved chunks?
* How would you present citations in the UI?
* What should happen if evidence is weak or incomplete?
* How would you support auditability?

---

## Q7. Complex questions

**How would you handle complex questions that require combining multiple guidance documents?**

### 想听到的点

* classify question type
* possible decomposition into sub-questions
* retrieve by subtask
* merge and deduplicate evidence
* structured answer generation
* uncertainty handling
* separate direct sources from inferred conclusions

### Follow-up

* Would you solve this in one prompt or multiple steps?
* What would the output structure look like?
* How would you handle conflicting guidance across documents?
* How would you respond if the answer depends on missing context?

---

## Q8. Standard RAG vs agentic / graph RAG

**When would standard RAG be sufficient, and when would you introduce agentic or graph-based orchestration?**

### 想听到的点

#### Standard RAG is enough for:

* direct factual lookup
* section-specific answers
* simple summaries
* straightforward guidance retrieval

#### More advanced orchestration may be needed for:

* multi-hop questions
* cross-document comparison
* policy hierarchy / document dependency
* scenario-based applicability questions
* combining policy/guidance with a second KB
* iterative clarification or decomposition

### Follow-up

* What is the risk of over-engineering with agents too early?
* What would you model as a graph?
* If graph extraction quality is weak, what would you do?
* Would you use a state machine or explicit workflow for certain task types?

---

## Q9. Dual knowledge base extension

**If later we add a second knowledge base containing case-specific evidence or submissions, how would you extend the design?**

### 想听到的点

* separate policy/guidance KB from evidence/submission KB
* query router or task classifier
* avoid blending normative guidance with case-specific facts
* structured answer that clearly distinguishes:

  * what the policy says
  * what the case evidence says
  * what gaps remain

### Follow-up

* How would you decide whether to query one KB or both?
* How would you prevent the model from incorrectly treating evidence as policy?
* How would you present the final answer clearly?

---

## Q10. Controls and risk

**What are the main risks in this product, and how would you control them?**

### 想听到的点

* hallucination
* weak retrieval
* outdated or superseded guidance
* wrong document precedence
* over-reliance by users
* poor ambiguity handling
* unsupported synthesis
* access control / sensitive content
* hidden prompt or orchestration failures

### Follow-up

* What should be logged?
* What should trigger “insufficient evidence” instead of an answer?
* How would you handle conflicting or overlapping guidance?
* What should remain a human judgement call?

---

## Q11. Evaluation

**How would you evaluate whether the product is working well?**

### 想听到的点

* benchmark question set
* retrieval quality
* citation correctness
* groundedness
* answer usefulness
* task-based evaluation
* ambiguity handling
* reviewer/SME acceptance
* regression testing

### Follow-up

* What would your offline evaluation set look like?
* How would you evaluate complex multi-document questions?
* What would a high “user correction” rate suggest?
* What metrics matter most in MVP versus later stages?

---

## Q12. Workstream leadership

**If you were leading this workstream, what would you aim to deliver in the first 8–12 weeks?**

### 想听到的点

* curated KB and ingestion pipeline
* one or two core workflows
* reliable citations
* answer schema
* simple UI
* evaluation dataset
* pilot group with reviewers / risk team
* phased roadmap rather than big-bang delivery

### Follow-up

* What team roles do you need?
* What are the biggest dependencies?
* How would you involve SMEs?
* What would you deliberately leave for phase 2?

---

# 7. High-value practical challenge questions

这些问题非常适合区分“会讲概念”和“真的做过”。

---

## Practical 1

**Suppose retrieval returns the relevant documents, but the final answer is still weak or misleading. How would you debug it?**

### 强答案通常会提到

* inspect chunking
* inspect reranking
* inspect prompt construction
* inspect context truncation
* inspect task classification
* inspect citation mapping
* inspect whether the wrong passages were emphasized

---

## Practical 2

**Suppose two guidance documents appear to conflict. How should the system behave?**

### 强答案

* do not force a false single answer
* surface both sources
* consider document precedence / version / effective date
* explain ambiguity
* possibly recommend escalation or human review

---

## Practical 3

**Suppose the user asks: “What guidance applies here, what are the likely risk flags, and what evidence should be requested first?” How would you process that?**

### 强答案

* treat as multi-part task
* decompose into:

  * applicable guidance
  * risk flags
  * missing evidence
* retrieve separately
* structure answer into sections
* label direct evidence versus inferred output

---

## Practical 4

**What would your response schema look like for a complex answer?**

### 理想字段示例

* direct_answer
* relevant_guidance
* cited_sources
* caveats
* missing_information
* escalation_recommendation
* evidence_strength / confidence
* trace_id

---

## Practical 5

**If stakeholders say they want “a ChatGPT for policy that answers anything,” how would you respond?**

### 强答案

* reset expectations
* define scope
* prioritize trust and traceability
* emphasize controlled use cases first
* explain risk of over-generalization

# 8. Scoring rubric

每项 1–5 分。

---

## Dimension 1 — Product understanding

**是否理解这是 internal risk/review teams 的 policy & guidance support tool，而不是普通 chat assistant**

### 5

Clearly frames it as a controlled, evidence-backed guidance support product for review workflows.

### 3

Understands document Q&A, but with limited workflow or governance depth.

### 1

Treats it as a generic chatbot over documents.

---

## Dimension 2 — Practical RAG design

**是否具备真正的 RAG implementation understanding**

### 5

Explains parsing, chunking, metadata, retrieval, reranking, citations, and failure modes concretely.

### 3

Reasonable conceptual understanding, but light on implementation detail.

### 1

High-level buzzwords only.

---

## Dimension 3 — Handling complex enterprise questions

**是否能处理复杂 multi-document / ambiguity / applicability 场景**

### 5

Can explain decomposition, evidence merging, ambiguity handling, and structured outputs clearly.

### 3

Some awareness, but limited depth.

### 1

Only handles simple lookup cases.

---

## Dimension 4 — Governance and controls

**是否具备 enterprise trust / audit / control mindset**

### 5

Covers citations, auditability, outdated guidance risk, ambiguity handling, and human judgement boundaries.

### 3

Mentions some controls but not systematically.

### 1

Minimal governance thinking.

---

## Dimension 5 — Workstream leadership

**是否具备 product/workstream lead 能力**

### 5

Clear MVP, prioritisation, team/dependency model, rollout, and stakeholder management.

### 3

Some roadmap thinking, but weak prioritisation.

### 1

Purely technical brainstorming without delivery shape.

# 9. Strong answer signals

强候选人通常会：

* 一开始就把它定义成 **controlled policy/guidance support product**
* 明确说这不是 generic chatbot
* 能区分 direct lookup、complex synthesis、applicability guidance 等任务类型
* 会讲 metadata、versioning、effective dates、document precedence
* 会强调 citation quality 和 evidence traceability
* 会提 insufficient evidence / ambiguity-safe behavior
* 会说 standard RAG 不必过度升级为 agent system
* 但也知道哪些 complex cases 需要 decomposition / graph / orchestration
* 会给出靠谱的 MVP 范围
* 会讲 evaluation dataset 和 pilot rollout

---

# 10. Weak answer signals

弱候选人通常会：

* 把它直接说成“向量数据库 + LLM”
* 不提 citations / traceability
* 不提 versions / effective dates
* 不提 conflicting guidance
* 不提 ambiguity-safe handling
* 复杂问题只会说“把多个 chunk 给模型总结”
* 想一开始就上 full agentic graph system，没有 MVP judgement
* 没有 delivery plan

---

# 11. Suggested wrap-up question

最后 3–5 分钟可以问：

**What is the biggest mistake to avoid when building this product for internal risk and review teams?**

强候选人常见回答会包括：

* treating it as a generic chatbot
* weak citation quality
* failing to manage outdated/superseded guidance
* over-automating interpretation
* no ambiguity-safe behavior
* trying to do too much in phase 1
